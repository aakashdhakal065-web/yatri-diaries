Blog Post 1: Tilicho Yatra

Himale Dine Path, Tilicho ko Nilo Pani ra Aatma ko Shanti Adhyaya 1: Kathmandu bata Marsyangdi Nadi ko Kinaari-Kinaari Jaba sahare jivan ko kolahol, gadi ko kan-khadai garne horn, ra daily routine bata wakka lagera man le euta shant ra nirjan thau khojda, mero paitala automatic himal tira badhnalagi halcha. Tyo din bihana bihana Kathmandu ko Gongabu bus park bata euta purano local bus chadera mero yatra suru vayo. Bus prithvi highway hudai Marsyangdi nadi ko kinaari-kinaari aagadi badhyo. Bato bhari dhunga, mato, ra kheer-jasto udo bageka nadiya haru herdai jada jivan ko asli saundarya ko anubhav huna thalyo. Besisahar pugda samma ma bus ko dhula ra thakabhari le purai thakayeko thiyo, tara bholi palta bata suru hune pedestrian (padal yatra) ko utsah le tyo thaka lai ekchin ma gayeb banayeko thiyo. Sathi sanga milera aafno pichadi heavy backpack bokie, lathi hatma lidai hami Jagat ra Chame gaun ko ukali tira lagyam. Bato ma bhetine sthaniy gurung ra lama sampraday ko manchhe haru ko hasilo muhar ra "Namaste!" vanne shabda le yatri ko aatma lai nai shital banauthryo. Paitala ma chhauda chhaudai bato ko dhunga haru sanga ladeko sathi ko haso ra thakabhari le garda nindrai laageko tyo pahilo rat aaja pani samjhanchu. Adhyaya 2: Manang Gaun: Tibetan Sabhyata ko Jhalak ra Chiso Hawa 3,500 meters vanda mathi ko uchaai ma raheko Manang gaun pugda samma ma hawa ekdam thado huna thalyo. Tya ko ghar haru dhuwanro dhunga ra mati bata banayeka thiyey, ra chhauma yak ko bachha haru charirakheka thiyey. Euta sano local tea-house (lodge) ma basera tato dudh chiya ra thakali dal-bhat khada ko tyo anubhav aaja pani aakha agadi aauxa. Rat ko samay ma guesthouse ko aagan ma basera mathi aakash tira herdai lamo katha haru sunne moka vayo. Tya euta sthaniy budha-buda (elderly person) sanga kura garda waha le vannu vayo: "Baboo, himal vaneko kewal pahad matra haina, yo ta man ko ahankar lai galhaune devta ko tapobhumi ho." Waha ko tyo euta vakye le mero man ma gahiro chhap chodyo. Tyo chiso rat ma kambal odhera sutauda bahira chalan thale ko hawako san-san awaj le jindagi kasto rahasyamay cha vanera sochna badhyata garyo. Adhyaya 3: Tilicho Lake: Bishwo ko Sabse Uchaai ma Raheko Nilo Samudra Bholi palta bhori bihana andhyarai ma uthira hami Tilicho Base Camp bata uchaai tira ukali lagyam. Yo bato bishwo ko sabse dherai risk ra sabse dherai sundar bato ma parcha. Uchaai dherai vayeko karon le saas ferna alikati garo huna thalyo (High Altitude Sickness ko dar le har ek paitala phuk-phukera rakhnu parthyo). Bistaari bistaari hidda hidda aanta euta danda ko tuppo bata tala herda mero aakha khula ko khula ra mukha banda garna nasakine vayo. Tyo thiyo—Tilicho Lake (4,919 meters)! Charai tira thula-thula himal ko chuchi haru, ra bich ma ekdam bishal, nila rang ko talau. Tyo pani yesto shanta ra safa thiyo ki aakash ko saphai tyo vitra prastuta dekhinthyo. Tya pugera aafno jivan ko dherai samasya haru tyo bishal prakriti agadi dherai sano lagna thalyo. Hami tehi kinar ma basera tyo nirmal pani ma aafno chhaya herda mann ekdam pawan vayo. Kehi der tya basda lagthyo ki yaha bata kahile farkerahi na jau. Adhyaya 4: Thorong La Pass ko Sangharsh ra Muktinath ko Darshan Tilicho bata firti aayera hami world famous Thorong La Pass (5,416 meters) katne yatra ma lagyam. Subha 4 baje rati minus temperature ma torch bale, snow boots lagera hami snow-covered trail ma aagadi badhyam. Hawa yesto thado thiyo ki gari hat ma lagayeko panja (gloves) le pani thadopan rokna sakirakheko thiyena. Tyo pass ko tuppo ma pugera jaba gham ko pehilo kiran seto himal ko chuchi ma pareko kiran dekhiyo, taba tyo golden color ko drishya dekhera hami duijana ananda le karayem. Tyo uchaai ma aafno des ko flag fulauda dherai garva lagyo. Tyo pass katai sakesi tala Mustang ko Muktinath valley tira jharyam. Muktinath mandir ko 108 wota dhara bata bageka chiso pani ma snan garera sabai thakabhari ra paap kshalan vako aabhash vayo. Tya bata Jomsom hudai Pokhara farkida bato ma Kali Gandaki nadi ko black stones (Shaligram) haru sanga kheleko samjhana ajhai pani taza cha. Adhyaya 5: Yatra ko Antima Shanti ra Sikai Kathamndu farkine bela bus ko khidki bata baki raheka pahad haru herdai jada, malai aabhas vayo—yo yatra kewal ghumne thau matra haina, yo ta aafno bhitro ko dar ra kamzori lai jitne euta path thiyo. Jiban vaneko pani euta lamo ukali-orali yatra ho, jaha chunauti jatti nai garo vaye pani dridha sankalpa ra sahasi man vaye jun sukai sikhhar pani chuna sakincha.
